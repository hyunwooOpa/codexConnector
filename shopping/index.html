Catalog design

This tracker is intentionally generic. Each published product stores a stable model key plus any useful MPN, EAN/GTIN, size, colour, capacity or other variant attributes. This prevents superficially similar marketplace listings from being merged.

Additions are published to shopping/data/products.json; price checks append to shopping/data/price-history.json.

Initial shopping sources

Netherlands: brand stores, bol, Coolblue, MediaMarkt, Praxis, Kieskeurig, Beslist and other reputable local retailers/comparators. Germany: brand stores, Kaufland, Otto, MediaMarkt/Saturn, Idealo, Geizhals and other reputable local retailers/comparators. Amazon country stores should be researched via web search/comparison evidence rather than direct Amazon access.
